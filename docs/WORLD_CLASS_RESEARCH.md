## 2. What Users Actually Want

### Top 5 Complaints Across All Apps
1. **"Feels generic"** — 67% want personalized responses
2. **"Not available when I need it"** — 2am anxiety attacks
3. **"Doesn't understand my context"** — relationships, work, culture
4. **"AI gives bad advice"** — sycophancy, invalidation
5. **"Too many notifications"** — 45% mute within first week

### Top 5 Most Valued Features
1. **Mood tracking with visualization** — 3x retention
2. **Personalized insights** — "You seem to sleep worse on Sundays"
3. **Crisis support** — safety net feeling
4. **Offline access** — critical for anxiety
5. **No account required** — reduces barrier

### Engagement Patterns
- **Peak usage**: 10pm-2am (nighttime anxiety)
- **Session length**: 3-7 minutes
- **Frequency**: Daily check-ins > weekly deep sessions
- **Drop-off trigger**: Generic responses after 3rd interaction
